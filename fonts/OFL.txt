Families with a Reserved Font Name ship exactly as their authors made them, only compressed to
WOFF2: IBM Plex Sans, Source Sans 3, Lora, Cascadia Code, Cinzel Decorative, Pirata One, Lexend
and Andika (full files from github.com/google/fonts), Merriweather (its authors' web fonts,
github.com/SorkinType/Merriweather) and OpenDyslexic (github.com/antijingoist/opendyslexic).
JetBrains Mono, Spectral and Cinzel are latin subsets via Fontsource. Every other family is
Google Fonts' latin file, with more scripts for Noto Sans, Tektur and Kalam.
