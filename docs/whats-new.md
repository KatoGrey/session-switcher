# Gemini joins, and all three can share a chat

- **Gemini chats, with Gemini 4 Argon.** On your Google AI plan, through Google's Antigravity CLI. Turn it on in Setup → Gemini: it installs the CLI and signs you in with your Google account, a click each.
- **Its own place.** A violet Gemini group in the side list, **New Gemini chat** on every project, and Ctrl+K understands "gemini chat in my project to …".
- **Like the others:** replies stream in, its file edits show (with the changes and Undo), and its chats keep their history and come back after a restart.
- **It sees pictures.** Attach or paste one and Gemini reads it, the same as Claude and Codex.
- **Asks before running commands.** In Edit files, a command Gemini wants to run becomes a card: **Allow** once, or **Always** (Full access for that chat).
- **Claude, Codex and Gemini in one chat.** Each assistant you've set up has its own pill under the message box. Click one, or start with @codex or @gemini, to write to it: it joins the first time, caught up on the chat. **All** (or @all) goes round in turn, the chat's own first, each one building on what the others said.
- **Gemini in the rest of the crew tools:** it can race or split the work with Claude or Codex, and take queued tasks.
